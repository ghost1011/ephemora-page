The platform

Self-Hostable

Full data sovereignty. Deploy on your infrastructure. No cloud dependency. No telemetry you cannot control.

Cryptographic Verification

Every execution is HMAC-attested — prove what ran, when, and with what result, for auditors and regulators. Currently: HMAC attestation, not a zero-knowledge proof; ZK-proof on roadmap.

Compliance-Ready Traces

Signed, canonical execution records (Ed25519 + RFC 8785) map to DORA, EU AI Act, and NIS2 evidence requirements. Roadmap: pre-built compliance bundles.

Auto-Close Execution

After execution, the sandbox is closed and the working directory is removed — post-execution auto-close, no persistence by default. No residual state, no forensic residue. What runs stays contained.

Harvest now, decrypt later

When future decryption is a possibility, unnecessary persistence becomes a liability.

Adversaries collect encrypted data today and wait for quantum computers to decrypt it. CISA, NSA, NIST and the UK NCSC describe this as an ongoing operation — not a future hypothetical. Ephemora reduces the exposure window by reducing the lifetime of the execution environment.

Reduce the attack surface by reducing the lifetime of the execution surface.
Minimize today's exposure to reduce tomorrow's risk.
Ephemora treats persistence as a security decision — not a default.

Ephemora complements post-quantum cryptography by controlling the lifetime of the execution environment. It does not replace PQC, and it does not claim to re-encrypt data. It reduces where and for how long sensitive workloads execute.
