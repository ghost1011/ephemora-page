The challenge

Why now?

Persistence is a harvest surface. Adversaries collect encrypted data today and wait for quantum decryption.

CISA, NSA, NIST and the UK NCSC describe harvest-now-decrypt-later as an ongoing operation — not a future hypothetical — and CNSA 2.0 timelines put the migration clock on regulated organizations today. Every persistent execution environment is a standing target; Ephemora treats persistence as a security decision, not a default, and reduces the exposure window by reducing the lifetime of the execution surface.

Ephemora complements post-quantum cryptography by controlling the lifetime of the execution environment. It does not replace PQC and does not claim to re-encrypt data.
